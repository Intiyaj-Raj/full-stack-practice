Hi! I’m Intiyaj Ansari. I come from a small village and belong to a farmer family. Growing up in a village, technology was not something we used every day. Most people around me were connected with farming and a simple lifestyle. But from a young age, I was always curious about computers and the internet. Whenever I got a chance to use a computer or a mobile phone, I tried to explore how things worked.

During my school days, this curiosity slowly turned into a strong interest. I started wondering how websites are created, how apps work, and how technology can solve real problems. Because of this interest, I decided to choose Computer Science Engineering (CSE) in college so I could learn more about computers and programming.

At the beginning of my college journey, learning programming was not easy for me. Coming from a village background, many things were new and sometimes difficult to understand. But instead of giving up, I kept learning step by step. I watched tutorials, practiced coding, and tried to build small things on my own.

While learning web development, I started with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. The moment I created my first web page and saw it working in a browser, it felt amazing. That experience motivated me to keep learning more about web development.

Later, I discovered the MERN Stack — MongoDB, Express.js, React, and Node.js. I realized that with these technologies, I could build complete web applications from the front-end to the back-end. This made me even more excited about becoming a developer.

To improve my skills, I started building projects. Some of the projects I created include a Netflix clone, an Office clone, and my personal portfolio website. Every project came with new challenges, bugs, and mistakes, but solving those problems helped me grow and gain confidence as a developer.

Coming from a farmer family has taught me the value of hard work, patience, and consistency. I try to follow the same values in my learning journey as a developer.

Today, my goal is to continue growing as a MERN Stack Developer, work on real-world applications, and create useful digital products that people enjoy using. I believe that with continuous learning and dedication, I can build a successful career in technology.
